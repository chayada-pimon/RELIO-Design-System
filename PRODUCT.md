# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Thai business owners, sales teams, and operations teams use RELIO while looking up customer and order information and deciding what to do next.

## Product Purpose

RELIO connects CRM and OMS work in one place, reducing context switching so teams can find related information quickly and act with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Clear, friendly, connected, and confident. The interface is calm and helpful without making business work feel rigid.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded cards, competing primary actions, isolated data, vague action labels, gradients, glass effects, glow, and large brand-colour page fills.

## Design Principles

- Make relationships between customers, orders, and documents visible.
- State what changed and show one clear next action.
- Guide with concise, human language.
- Keep operational work calm, predictable, and easy to scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Thai is the default language and English is a complete alternative. Keep focus visible, provide 44 px interactive targets, do not convey state with colour alone, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
